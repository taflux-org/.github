# taflux

**Knowledge that people and AI can rely on.**

taflux is an open-source knowledge base for people and AI. It makes the
status, authority and access rights of every knowledge block explicit
--- so AI systems can use current, approved information without hiding
gaps or guessing.

**AI prepares. People decide.**

## What makes taflux different?

-   **Current knowledge:** Reviews and change relationships help
    identify outdated or superseded information.
-   **Human authority:** People confirm what is valid; confirmations are
    traceable.
-   **Clear context:** Each knowledge block records validity, trust
    level, bindingness, confidentiality, retention purpose and AI
    autonomy.
-   **Visible gaps:** Missing, outdated or restricted information is
    made explicit.
-   **Access by design:** AI systems only search the knowledge their
    access policy permits.
-   **Portable format:** Knowledge is stored as Markdown in Git, based
    on the Open Knowledge Format (OKF).

## Components

-   **Editor:** An OKF-based Markdown editor with review and merge
    workflows.
-   **MCP server:** Connects taflux to compatible AI systems and
    searches by keyword and meaning.
-   **Team workflows:** Named responsibility, confirmations and
    configurable access.

taflux complements existing document storage; it does not replace it.
